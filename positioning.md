# 6sense Brand Positioning

## Positioning Statement

6sense is the leading revenue intelligence platform that empowers B2B organizations to achieve predictable revenue growth by leveraging AI-powered insights to identify, prioritize, and engage in-market accounts at the right time with the right message.

## Key Messages

1. **Revenue AI for the entire buying journey** — 6sense uses artificial intelligence and big data to uncover anonymous buying signals, predict account behavior, and recommend the best actions to drive pipeline and revenue.
2. **Eliminate guesswork from B2B sales and marketing** — The platform replaces intuition-driven outreach with data-driven precision, ensuring teams focus only on accounts most likely to convert.
3. **Unified ABM platform** — 6sense consolidates account-based marketing, sales intelligence, and predictive analytics into a single platform, eliminating tool sprawl and data silos.
4. **Dark funnel visibility** — 6sense uniquely captures anonymous intent data (the "dark funnel") to surface accounts that are actively researching solutions but have not yet engaged directly.
5. **Predictable pipeline and revenue** — The platform's core promise is turning unpredictable revenue processes into predictable, repeatable outcomes through data and AI.

## Value Propositions

- **AI-Powered Revenue Intelligence:** Leverage patented AI models to identify in-market accounts, predict buying stage, and recommend next-best actions.
- **Dark Funnel Insights:** Capture and analyze anonymous buyer intent signals that competitors cannot see, giving sales and marketing a first-mover advantage.
- **Account-Based Everything:** Orchestrate coordinated campaigns across advertising, email, sales outreach, and web personalization — all targeting the same prioritized accounts.
- **Predictable Pipeline Generation:** Replace hope-based forecasting with data-driven pipeline creation, improving conversion rates and shortening sales cycles.
- **Revenue Team Alignment:** Unite marketing, sales, and customer success around shared account intelligence, eliminating handoff friction and conflicting priorities.

## Tone & Voice

- **Confident and authoritative** — Speaks as the established category leader in revenue intelligence and ABM.
- **Data-driven and precise** — Messaging emphasizes measurable outcomes, specific metrics, and quantifiable results (e.g., "2x pipeline," "40% faster deal cycles").
- **Approachable but professional** — Avoids overly casual language but remains accessible; not stiff or academic.
- **Forward-looking and innovative** — Positions the brand at the frontier of AI and revenue technology; uses language that signals sophistication and next-generation capability.
- **Customer-outcome oriented** — Consistently frames features in terms of business results rather than technical specifications.

## Target Audience

- **Primary:** VP and C-level leaders in B2B marketing, sales, and revenue operations at mid-market and enterprise companies.
- **Secondary:** Demand generation managers, ABM practitioners, and sales development leaders who influence platform purchase decisions.
- **Industry focus:** Technology, SaaS, financial services, manufacturing, and professional services — sectors with complex, long-cycle B2B sales motions.
- **Pain points addressed:**
  - Wasted spend on accounts that are not in-market
  - Inability to see or act on anonymous buyer research
  - Misalignment between sales and marketing on target accounts
  - Unpredictable pipeline and revenue forecasting
  - Tool sprawl across point solutions for intent, ABM, and analytics

## Competitive Differentiation

- **vs. Demandbase:** 6sense emphasizes its proprietary AI engine and dark funnel data capture as superior to Demandbase's approach; positions itself as more predictive and less reliant on third-party data.
- **vs. Bombora:** 6sense offers a full-platform solution (not just intent data), combining Bombora-like intent signals with orchestration, activation, and analytics in one product.
- **vs. ZoomInfo:** 6sense differentiates by focusing on account-level buying signals and predictive intelligence rather than contact-level data enrichment.
